No hardware was operated or tested while preparing this CAD package.

1. Verify the G7SA-2A2B DC12 part marking, direct-PCB pin orientation, solder
   joints, buck assembly and all connector polarities with supplies off.
2. Check K1 continuity: coil off closes only NC 11-12 and 21-22; external 12 V
   closes only NO 33-34 and 43-44. Check coil isolation from all power returns.
   The manufacturer's forcibly guided mechanism does not replace verification
   of wiring, assembly or the system's required fault response.
3. Test isolated 5 V, nominal 36 V, UVLO and ripple using a current-limited
   input supply and dummy load before connecting the motor PCB. Limit current
   to the documented rating of the actual jumper leads and mating contacts,
   never above the selected header's 3 A maximum. No unspecified jumper rating
   is assumed. The retained 10 A fuses are not jumper-overload protection.
4. Check external one-second AC timing and drive inhibition before transfer.
   Check active-low J11 and always-on auxiliary outputs. J11 senses the command,
   not the contact position. It cannot detect a welded contact or unplugged wire.
5. Characterize contact voltage/current and capacitive inrush in both directions
   of transfer. Zero motor load is not zero capacitor making current. The relay's
   6 A / 30 VDC resistive switching rating does not establish safe repeated
   switching of this 36-54.6 V capacitive circuit. Manufacturer load confirmation
   and measured inrush are required before a loaded transfer rating is assigned.
6. Scope startup, input dips, load steps, turn-off and transfer. Use differential
   or appropriately isolated probing so scope grounds do not bridge domains.
7. Measure U5, L1, D7, relay, fuses, headers, mating sockets and wire temperature
   at the intended steady load and peak duration. No thermal or current rating
   for the assembled board is inferred from a clean CAD check.
8. The converter's current threshold is not a precision 3 A harness limiter.
   Characterize the limited-energy VM clamp separately. Its roughly 0.31 A sink
   cannot absorb sustained backdrive and does not guarantee a 40 V ceiling.
9. Validate actual firmware inhibition and phase-current limits. The final motor
   PCB has not changed; this intermediate revision does not increase its ratings.

Retained Rev L checks for the sourcing changes
- C18/C20/C21 are 270 uF each. An ideal 2.8 ms ramp needs about 7.04 A into the
  two converter-side bulk caps with K1 open, or 10.55 A with K1 closed before
  adding motor-board capacitance. Those are charging-demand calculations, not
  measured pulses or an output rating. Actual current limiting can extend the
  ramp. Verify no restart loop/hiccup and check startup at input/load extremes.
- Closing K1 can transfer energy from charged C20 directly into discharged
  C21/motor capacitance. The buck current limit does not limit that initial
  capacitor-to-capacitor pulse. Measure it and qualify the relay before repeated
  transfer. The retained no-precharge choice does not establish inrush safety.
- Internal compensation (R13 100k) is retained. No numerical phase-margin claim
  is made. Check load-step ringing/settling and startup with the intended wiring,
  input supply and motor load; use loop-gain measurement for a qualified release.
- Exact TDK DC-bias effective capacitance could not be confirmed from the blocked
  characteristic download. Do not assume 4.7 uF remains unchanged at operating
  voltage. Verify high-frequency input/output ripple and MLCC temperature rise.
- Check L1 temperature and saturation under the intended load. Its 11 A / 20%
  saturation-drop value is a 25 C typical characteristic, not a hot-fault rating.
  Keep bench current within the actual harness rating, never above 3 A headers.


Rev N SOC sensing checks (dummy loads first)
10. Check R33/R34 polarity and continuity: positive current is battery OUT through
    R33 and charger INTO the relay through R34. Confirm all battery-side loads,
    including isolated 5 V and sensor LDO, are downstream of R33.
11. With host disconnected, test U10 output (~3.31 V) and temperature from the
    intended input range. C29 must retain >=1 uF effective capacitance; C30+C31
    must retain >=4.7 uF at operating bias/temperature. Nominal values are 4.7 uF
    and 20 uF respectively. Validate LDO startup/stability and local decoupling.
12. With J12 disconnected, power the normal isolated 5 V path. Verify U11
    input at C36 is about 5 V and output at C37 is about 3.3 V, referenced to
    SYSTEM_RET. No external 3.3 V supply is connected. Verify startup, shutdown,
    ripple and temperature at expected load; C36/C37 must each retain >0.47 uF
    effective capacitance. Nominal values are 10 uF / 16 V each.
    Power the host from its normal 5 V harness, then connect J12 with the SOC
    firmware configured. Confirm U7 0x40 and U8 0x41 device IDs and bounded I2C.
    Test both relay states, resets, battery loss and stuck SDA/SCL. Test any
    proposed independent host-power sequence before using it; powered pull-ups
    may back-power an unpowered host through its I/O protection.
13. Calibrate each sensor with a known current and meter reference; measure zero
    offset separately. U7 normally reads auxiliary consumption even with motor
    off. Do not zero this real load away. Use a controlled fixture or independently
    measure that load when determining U7 offset. Reverse-current sign must work.
14. Verify net coulomb balance during drive, charging and idle. Compare integrated
    Ah with a calibrated external instrument; verify no double counting of power
    or loss. Drop invalid/stale samples and report uncertainty after downtime.
15. Test installed firmware drive inhibition first, then SOC/UART telemetry while
    the motor control loop runs. Reassign PA5 before connecting J12. Require no
    control-loop timing degradation, no unintended gate enable and sane recovery.
Use differential/isolated instrumentation across the barrier. Ordinary bench
scope channel ground clips are common and must not short separate returns.

Rev O relay-supply pass-through checks
16. With all power off, verify J5.1-J14.1 continuity and J5.2-J14.2 continuity,
    no cross-short, and no COIL_RET connection to battery/SYSTEM_RET. Connect
    J14.1 to motor J11.1 Relay+, J14.2 to J11.2 Relay-. Check actual part markings
    confirm all five motor relay coils are 12 V before applying this source.
17. Use one switched 12 V source, rated >=0.3 A (prefer 0.5 A). With drive/charge
    power disabled, measure total coil current and voltage at both boards while
    all coils are energized. Check jumper polarity, contact heating and pickup.
18. Verify the source driver's and motor board's existing coil suppression,
    measure 12 V turn-off transients and relay release time. D3/D4 protect only
    the intermediate branch; J14 is before D3 and has no added clamp. Verify
    actual contact sequencing remains compatible with the retained one-second
    AC lead/lag. No AC or VM power is applied for initial coil-wiring checks.
