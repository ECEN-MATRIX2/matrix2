REV O - SENSING FLOW AND ISOLATION

Battery XT60 -> F1 -> R33 -> buck -> linked relay NC -> motor VM
                         -> isolated 5 V converter (always on)
                         -> local battery-domain sensor LDO
Charger input -> R34 -> linked relay NO -> battery charge port

U7 measures battery bus voltage and R33 drop. It runs on BAT_P_RET with its
own 3.31 V LDO. SDA/SCL pass through U9 ISO1640 to the host SYSTEM_RET domain.
U8 measures charge-source voltage and R34 drop, powered from local U11 3.3 V and
referenced to the motor/charger SYSTEM_RET. It shares the host I2C side.
J12 therefore returns both monitors' registers over one pair of signal wires.
U11 derives SENSE_3V3 from ISO_5V / SYSTEM_RET. J13 is removed; no host supply
wire is needed. Existing 5 V harnesses supply the system reference return.
U10 still supplies BAT_SENSE_3V3 / BAT_P_RET. No sensing wire bridges the
battery-side isolation barrier or shorts the isolated coil return.

This preserves the previously accepted MODE-SWITCHED ground arrangement.
In drive, NC contacts connect battery/buck return to SYSTEM_RET; NO charge
contacts are open. In charge, NC opens both VM feed wires and NO connects the
charge path. The motor PCB already shares VM/charge/MCU ground. This design
does not claim all three returns remain permanently isolated in both modes.
The digital isolator prevents the battery sensor link adding an extra return
bridge around the drive contacts. Spacing is functional low-voltage isolation,
not mains-rated certification. Scope/programmer grounds must not bypass it.

SOC uses I_charge - I_battery_draw, integrated over elapsed time. This includes
buck losses and the intermediate board's auxiliary draw. Internal BMS drain,
unconnected external loads and periods without valid sampling are not counted.
Capacity, starting SOC, current offsets and gain require calibration. Rested
voltage can correct long-term drift; voltage under load/charge alone is weak.
See FIRMWARE_INTEGRATION.txt for the proposed MCU host and GNSS-preserving UART.

33 additional fitted parts relative to Rev L:
  5 ICs: two INA237, one ISO1640, one TPS7A4001, one TLV75533
  2 precision 5 milliohm shunts
  6 series filter resistors, 4 I2C pull-ups, 2 LDO set resistors
  13 capacitors for local supply and input filtering
  1 two-pin 2.54 mm header (J12)
Six new Mouser SKUs relative to Rev L; other additions reuse Rev L stocked part selections.

No current rating increase follows from sensing. The existing 3 A header limit
and the actual harness rating still apply, separately from the motor PCB phase
relay limitations. 10 A fuses are retained as requested, not as jumper protection.

Rev O adds one relay-supply pass-through header, J14, outside the sensing
circuit. The 33 sensing/supply additions above remain unchanged; total fitted
additions versus Rev L are now 34. J14 does not add an SOC measurement or ground.
