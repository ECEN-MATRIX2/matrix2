The two previously reported slot features are not present in Rev O. The XT60
footprint uses circular 4.50 mm drills; the smallest via drill is 0.30 mm, not
0.20 mm. Exported tools: 0.30, 0.40, 1.00, 1.40, 3.20, 4.50 mm. NPTH mounting
holes are separately identified. No filled/capped vias or impedance control
are specified as special processes. Have the assembler review U5 paste and
ordinary plated through-via solder wicking before selecting its stencil.

Manufacturing/Gerbers contains nine Gerber X2 files, the .gbrjob layer map,
and separate PTH/NPTH Excellon files with drill maps. Use the copper layers
in the order above; do not omit inner layers or treat the drill map as a drill
program. Edge_Cuts defines the final outline. Paste files are in Assembly,
separate from the bare-board order. Quantities/finishes must be selected in
the vendor order; CAD checks are not confirmation of vendor CAM acceptance.

The nominal dielectric subdivision in KiCad is for display. The vendor may
use its standard .062-inch four-layer stack while keeping the layer order
and 1 oz copper. No special 0.20 mm prepreg thickness or controlled impedance
is requested. In1 is the close ground reference; confirm the standard stack
does not place it unusually far from the top switching circuitry.

Assembly notes for selected replacements
TDK CNC6 soft-termination capacitors require reflow and correct underside
orientation. Pads use a 2.2 mm inner gap, 1.1 mm length and 2.5 mm width per the
TDK recommended ranges. No optional wash slit is fitted; the process prohibits
slots. Use a suitable no-clean reflow process or validate complete cleaning and
drying under the parts. Follow component reflow profiles and moisture handling.
L1 and R16 use their exact manufacturer land dimensions, not generic packages.
Assembly_Top.svg and Placement_SMD.csv accompany the paste layers. The assembler
must validate component orientation, paste and through-hole assembly sequence.

SOC assembly additions
U10 thermal pad must be soldered to battery-return copper. Ordinary through
vias are used; no filled/capped via requirement is imposed. Review paste and
solder-wicking with the assembler. R33/R34 use lead-free WSL25125L000FEA18
with 2.11 x 3.68 mm pads and 3.18 mm inner gap, plus separate sense pickups.
U7/U8 are 0.5 mm-pitch VSSOP; the 0.16 mm PCB rules are retained.

Rev O adds two ordinary 1.00 mm circular PTH holes for J14. All other
